**RE: Application to Discharge [or Modify] Restrictive Covenant**
**Property:** [CIVIC ADDRESS]
**PID:** [PID NUMBER]
**Covenant Instrument:** [INSTRUMENT NUMBER], registered [DATE], Grantor: [NAME]

**1. Subject Property**
The subject property is located at [CIVIC ADDRESS], PID [NUMBER], in the [ZONE DESIGNATION] zone. The property is currently used as [current use].

**2. The Restrictive Covenant**
A restrictive covenant was registered against the subject property on [DATE] as Instrument [NUMBER] by [GRANTOR NAME]. The relevant restricting clauses are as follows:

- **Clause [X]:** "[Quote exact text of restricting clause]"
- **Clause [Y]:** "[Quote exact text of any additional restricting clause being targeted]"

**3. Covenant is More Restrictive Than Current Zoning**
The subject property is currently zoned [ZONE] under the [applicable Land Use Bylaw]. The [ZONE] zone permits [state maximum permitted density — e.g., up to 4 dwelling units; or up to 6 storeys at X units/hectare]. The covenant restricts the property to [single-family residential / single dwelling / etc.], which is more restrictive than the current zoning with respect to [density / height].

Accordingly, the covenant satisfies the test for administrative discharge under Section 257A of the HRM Charter.

**4. Proposed Modification / Discharge**

*[CHOOSE APPLICABLE OPTION]*
*[OPTION A — Full discharge]:* We request discharge of the restrictive covenant in its entirety.
*[OPTION B — Partial discharge]:* We request discharge of Clauses [X] and [Y] only, as these are the provisions that are more restrictive than current zoning with respect to density. The remaining covenant clauses are not targeted by this application.

**5. Effect of Discharge**
If discharged, all future development of the property will be governed by the applicable Land Use Bylaw and applicable municipal regulations. We note that HRM does not enforce private covenants when processing permit applications; however, discharge provides certainty to the owner and any prospective financiers.

Please find enclosed: (i) a certified copy of the covenant instrument; (ii) supporting title documentation.
